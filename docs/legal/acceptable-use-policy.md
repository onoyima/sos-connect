# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft for legal review — version {{VERSION}}.** Prepared as a working template. Before publishing, have a lawyer qualified in each country where you operate review and adapt it, and fill every [bracketed] item.

You must not use SOS Connect to:

1. Harass, bully, threaten, stalk, intimidate or discriminate against anyone.
2. Share sexual content involving anyone under 18 (we report this to the authorities), or any sexual content without consent.
3. Share other people's personal data (photos, phone numbers, addresses, grades, health information) without a lawful reason.
4. Impersonate someone, including staff, offices or other institutions.
5. Raise false SOS alerts or safety-check answers, or misuse responder tools.
6. Send spam, chain messages, scams or malware; or advertise without your institution's permission.
7. Incite violence, promote terrorism, or share content that is illegal in your country.
8. Interfere with the service: probing, scraping, breaking encryption, tampering with or flooding the offline relay, or using another person's device keys.
9. Record calls without the other person's knowledge where the law requires consent.

Report abuse from any message (**Report**). Your institution reviews reports and may remove content, suspend accounts, and apply its disciplinary rules. We may act directly where there is a risk to safety or a legal requirement.
