This policy explains how SOS Connect handles personal data of students, staff and guests of institutions that use it. SOS Connect is operated by **{{OPERATOR}}**, {{ADDRESS}} ("we"). Contact our Data Protection Officer at **{{DPO_EMAIL}}**.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Your institution is the data controller** for your membership data and for what happens in its SOS Connect space (chats, SOS alerts, announcements, safety checks). It decides who may use the service and why.
- **We are the institution's data processor.** We process that data only on the institution's documented instructions, under a Data Processing Agreement.
- **We are an independent controller** only for: running and securing the platform (security logs, abuse prevention), institution administrator accounts, billing, and meeting our own legal obligations.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership | name, student/staff number, official email, role, faculty, department, level, active/inactive status, whether you are under 18 | your institution's systems, at sign-in |
| Content | messages, files, voice notes, status updates, announcements you post | you |
| Safety | SOS alerts (category, your note, time), location if you allowed it, safety-check answers, responder actions | you and responders |
| Calls | who called whom, when, how long (not the audio or video) | the service |
| Device | device identifier, platform, public encryption keys, push-notification token | your phone |
| Technical | IP address, sign-in times, security logs | the service |

We **do not** receive or store your institution password. It is sent once, encrypted, to your institution's own login system, which tells us only whether it is correct. We do **not** collect contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or your location outside of an SOS or a safety-check answer you choose to send. Your date of birth, if your institution sends it, is used only to tell whether you are under 18 and is not stored.

## 3. Why we use it and our legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis (NDPA s.25 / GDPR art.6) |
| --- | --- |
| Giving you chat, calls, groups and announcements within your institution | performance of the institution's arrangement with you; the institution's legitimate interests / public task |
| Sending and handling SOS alerts, including location | vital interests (protecting life); your choice to attach location is recorded |
| Passing encrypted messages and alerts phone-to-phone when there is no internet | legitimate interests (keeping people reachable in emergencies), with encryption so relaying phones cannot read them |
| Security, preventing abuse, audit logs | legitimate interests; legal obligation |
| Responding to your rights requests and to regulators | legal obligation |

We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Other members** of your institution see your name, role and department. Your email and phone are shown only to staff. Statuses are visible only to the audience you choose.
- **Responders** (campus security and other roles your institution appoints) see SOS alerts with your name, contact details and location so they can reach you.
- **Members near you when you are offline** receive your SOS alert (name, category, your note and location if attached) so they can help or pass it on. Members of other institutions may carry your alert but cannot read it.
- **Institution administrators** can manage accounts, read content that has been reported to them, and see SOS records. They cannot silently read private chats through SOS Connect.
- **Our staff** access personal data only to provide support or keep the service secure, under confidentiality duties and with access logged.
- **Service providers** we use (listed in our Sub-processors page), bound by contract.
- **Authorities**, only when required by a valid legal order, or when necessary to protect someone's life. We will tell the institution unless the law forbids it.

## 5. Offline (mesh) messaging

When there is no internet, your phone exchanges encrypted packages with nearby phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and may keep other people's encrypted packages for up to 3 days to pass them on. Each package is signed so it cannot be forged or changed. Direct messages are encrypted so only the recipient's phone can open them; group messages and SOS alerts are encrypted with your institution's key, so only members of your institution can read them. You can see what your phone is carrying on the **Nearby** screen.

## 6. How long we keep data

See the Data Retention Schedule. In short: messages 12 months, statuses 24 hours, SOS records 24 months (precise location blurred to about 1 km after 30 days), call logs 6 months, security/audit logs 24 months. Your institution may set shorter periods.

## 8. Security

TLS for all traffic; encryption of message content at rest; signed and encrypted offline packages; passwords never stored; per-institution separation enforced in every database query; role-based access with audit logs; suspended accounts lose access immediately and their phones lose the ability to read new offline traffic.

## 9. Children

Many students are 18 or older; some may not be. If you are under 18, your institution must confirm a parent or guardian's consent (NDPA s.31; GDPR art.8 where it applies) before chat features are enabled. SOS remains available to everyone. See the Children & Members Under 18 page.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your country you may: be informed; access your data and get a copy (use **My data & privacy → Download my data**); correct it; have it erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; withdraw consent where we rely on it (for example, turning off location for SOS); and complain to a regulator. We answer within **30 days**. Because your institution is the controller, we may pass your request to it.

Regulators: Nigeria — Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC); Ghana — Data Protection Commission; Kenya — Office of the Data Protection Commissioner; South Africa — Information Regulator; EU/UK — your local supervisory authority.

## 11. Changes

We will notify you in the app before material changes take effect and ask you to accept them again where the law requires it.
