# Children & Members Under 18

> **Draft for legal review — version {{VERSION}}.** Prepared as a working template. Before publishing, have a lawyer qualified in each country where you operate review and adapt it, and fill every [bracketed] item.

Some students and guests may be under 18. The NDPA treats anyone under 18 as a child (s.31, read with the Child's Rights Act 2003) and requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian, with appropriate verification. GDPR article 8 and POPIA s.35 set similar rules where they apply.

## How SOS Connect handles it
1. **Identification.** If the institution's connector sends a date of birth (or an under-18 flag), SOS Connect marks the member as under 18. The date itself is not stored.
2. **Consent.** The institution confirms guardian consent — usually already collected at admission — in the admin panel (*Members → under 18 → consent needed*), stating its source. The confirmation is recorded with who confirmed it and when.
3. **Until consent is recorded,** the member can sign in and use **SOS and safety checks** (protecting life is a recognised basis); chat, status, calls and the directory stay switched off automatically.
4. **Extra protections for under-18 members:** their status updates are limited to contacts; staff cannot see their phone numbers outside SOS; reports involving them are prioritised; content sexualising minors is removed and reported to the authorities.
5. **Rights** may be exercised by the child or by the parent/guardian.

Institutions must not send data of children who are not their students, and must tell parents/guardians how the service works.
