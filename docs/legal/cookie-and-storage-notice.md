# Cookie & Device Storage Notice

> **Draft for legal review — version {{VERSION}}.** Prepared as a working template. Before publishing, have a lawyer qualified in each country where you operate review and adapt it, and fill every [bracketed] item.

**Website.** We use only strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and an XSRF cookie that protects forms against cross-site attacks. We store your chosen institution in your browser so you don't have to search for it again. No analytics or advertising cookies.

**Phone app.** The app stores, on your phone only: your sign-in token and device keys (in the phone's secure keystore), your conversations, messages and alerts (in an app database), and encrypted packages it is carrying for others. Signing out deletes all of it from the phone.
