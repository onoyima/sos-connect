---
title: Platform chat (admins and the platform team)
audience: admins
---
The round headset button at the bottom right of the website opens the **platform chat**. It is only there for school admins (people who can change their school's settings) and for the platform team.

## Who can talk to whom
* **Platform team:** message each other, and any school's admins. Pick one person for a one-to-one chat, or several (even from different schools) for a group, and give the group a name if you like.
* **School admins:** message the platform team for help with setup, sign-in or anything else, and reply in any group the platform team adds you to.
* Students, staff and parents don't see the platform chat, and it is separate from your school's own chats.

## Using it
* The number on the button shows unread messages. New messages arrive straight away; if live updates are down they still show within a minute.
* **Enter** sends, **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.
* People are shown by name and by school (or "Platform team"); roles are never shown.
