## Where things are
* **Admin → Features:** switch features on or off for everyone (calls, video, group calls, voice notes, stickers, status reactions, mute/archive/pause, pop-ups, face capture…).
* **Admin → Roles:** what each role may do and where (a faculty, department or unit). People only see their own role.
* **Admin → Members:** find people, give roles or single permissions, suspend, record guardian consent.
* **Admin → Groups:** every group; make or remove group admins (also from *Group info* inside a chat).
* **Admin → Pop-ups and marquee:** a pop-up shows every time people open the app until you pause it; a marquee line slides across the top. Choose who sees each (everyone, students, staff, a faculty, department, unit or hostel, a level).
* **Admin → Face capture:** progress, each person's photos, and sending them to the school (encrypted file or the school database).
* **Admin → Settings:** records connection, sign-in formats, under-18 consent switch, favicon and chat icon, seasonal icons.
* **Help** and the **design document** can be edited by admins with the "Edit help pages" permission.
