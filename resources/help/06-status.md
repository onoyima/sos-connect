---
title: Status updates
audience: everyone
---
A status lasts **24 hours**. It can be text on a colour, a photo, a GIF, a video or audio.

## Who sees it
Choose when you share:
* **People I have chatted with** (default) · **My department** · **My faculty**
* **Groups I choose** (members of groups you're in) · **People I choose**
* **Everyone in my institution** (if your institution allows it)

Use **Hide from** to keep it from particular people, whatever the audience. People you **tag** can see it and are told.

## Reacting
Like or love a status, comment and reply to comments, or reply privately (it goes to your one-to-one chat). On your own status you can see who viewed it.
