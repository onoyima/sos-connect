## Your groups appear by themselves
When you sign in you're added to the groups that match your records: your university, faculty, department, level, courses, hostel (and alumni groups after you graduate). Staff join their unit's staff groups.

## Starting a chat
Press **New chat** (web) or the **New chat** button (phone), search for a name, then:
* **Chat**: you can message them straight away.
* **Send request**: they must accept first (see *Chat requests*).

## In a chat
* **Reply:** hover over (web) or swipe/long-press (phone) a message and choose Reply.
* **Mention someone:** type **@** and their name. They're told even if they muted the group.
* **Voice notes, emojis and stickers:** use the buttons beside the message box.
* **Edit or delete:** your own messages, within 15 minutes for edits.
* **Search a chat:** the magnifying glass at the top.

## Group info
Tap a group's name to see members and, if you're a group admin, add people, make someone a group admin, or remove them.
