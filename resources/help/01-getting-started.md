---
title: Getting started and signing in
audience: everyone
---
SOS Connect is your institution's chat, calls and safety app. It works on the website and on the phone app, and the phone app keeps working without internet.

## Signing in
* **Students:** your **matric number** and your **school portal password**.
* **Staff:** your staff email (or staff number) and your portal password.
* SOS Connect checks your password with your school's records. It never keeps a copy. To change or reset it, use your school portal.

## One phone and one computer
Your account can be open on **one phone and one web browser** at the same time. If you sign in on another phone, the old phone is signed out; the same goes for browsers. You'll see a message saying why.

## Using it on a computer
On the computer's sign-in page choose **Link with phone**, then on your phone open **Me → Linked devices → Link a web browser** and point the camera at the code. You can also use your password.

## Signing out
Choose **Sign out** in your account menu (web) or **Me → Sign out** (phone). You'll be asked to confirm.
