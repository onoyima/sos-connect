---
title: Face capture
audience: everyone
---
If your institution uses face capture, open it from your account menu (web) or **Me → Face capture** (phone).

1. Read and accept what the photos are used for.
2. Follow the spoken and written prompts: look straight, blink, turn left, turn right, look up, look down, open your mouth, look straight again.
3. Each photo is taken for you when it's right and appears at the bottom as it's saved.

**Tips:** face a window or a lamp; remove sunglasses, caps and masks (normal glasses are fine); hold the phone at eye level, at arm's length.

Under-18s need a parent's or guardian's consent recorded first. You can delete your face photos from the same page; copies the school already received are handled by the school.
