---
title: Voice, video and group calls
audience: everyone
---
* Start a call with the phone or camera button at the top of a chat.
* In a group, the same buttons start a **group call**; others see *Call in progress* with a **Join** button.
* Incoming calls ring on the website and the phone with **Answer** and **Decline**. Missed calls show as a red count on the phone icon.
* Calls need internet (or, on the phone app, the same Wi-Fi as the other person).
* If the camera or microphone doesn't work, allow them for this site in your browser settings, or for the app in your phone settings.
