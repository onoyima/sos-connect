---
title: Using the phone app without internet
audience: everyone
---
* Your chats and groups are kept on your phone, so you can read them offline.
* Messages you send offline are passed **phone to phone** over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi to people nearby, and delivered properly as soon as any phone in the chain gets internet.
* Phones on the same Wi-Fi (even with no internet) can message and call each other.
* Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on and allow the app's "nearby devices" permission. See **Nearby phones** at the top of Chats.
* Everything is signed and encrypted, so phones that carry your message can't read or change it.
