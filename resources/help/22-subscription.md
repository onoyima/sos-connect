title: Your subscription (admins)
audience: admins
---
**Admin → Subscription** shows your institution's SOS Connect plan, when it renews (or when your free trial ends), how many students and staff you have against your plan's limit, and your invoices.

## Paying
* **Pay online** opens a secure Paystack page (card, bank transfer or USSD). The subscription updates as soon as the payment goes through.
* **Bank transfer:** use the bank details on the page and put the **invoice number** as the reference. The SOS Connect team records it when it arrives.

## Changing plan
Pick a plan (yearly or monthly) and choose it. You get an invoice for it; the new plan starts when it's paid, and any time you have already paid for carries on. A free plan starts straight away.

## Things to know
* **SOS always works**, in every plan, even if a payment is late.
* A banner reminds you a few days before a trial or period ends, and when a payment is due.
* If a payment is overdue for longer than the grace period, most features pause until it's paid. SOS, notices and safety checks keep working.
* If you reach your plan's member limit, everyone already signed in carries on, but new students and staff can't sign in until you move to a bigger plan.
* **Features** shows which features your plan includes; the others are marked "Not in your plan".
