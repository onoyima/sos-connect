audience: everyone

To keep the campus safe from unwanted messages, some people must **ask first**:

| From | To | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff | Any student | Straight away |
| Staff | Staff in the same department, faculty or unit | Straight away |
| Staff | Staff elsewhere | Chat request |
| Student | Anyone | Chat request |

Heads and offices given the right to (for example registry or student affairs) can message people in their area straight away. Calls follow the same rules.

## Sending and answering
* Search for the person, choose **Send request**, and add a short note saying who you are.
* Requests waiting for you show at the top of your chats with **Accept** and **Decline**.
* When someone answers your request you're told, and the answer shows at the top of your chats (red count) until you've seen it.
