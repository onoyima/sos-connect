## Raising an SOS
Press the red **SOS** button and choose what's happening. Campus security, the clinic and people nearby are alerted at once. If you allowed it, your location is attached.

**Without internet** the phone app passes your SOS to nearby phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi until one of them reaches the internet. Always also call the emergency numbers shown in the app if you can.

## "Are you safe?" checks
During an incident your institution may ask whether you're safe. Answer from the banner or the Safety page so responders know who needs help.

## Notices, pop-ups and the sliding line
Important notices appear in **Notices**. Your institution may also show a **pop-up** when you open the app, or a **line of text sliding across the top**. It never blocks your chats or calls; tap ✕ to hide it.
