---
title: Mute, archive and pause a chat
audience: everyone
---
Open the chat's menu (**⋮** at the top of the chat, the arrow beside it in the list on the web, or a long press on the phone):

* **Mute** for 8 hours, a week or always: no alerts or red counts, but new messages still show in the chat.
* **Archive:** moves the chat into **Archived** at the top of your list. Open *Archived* to see or unarchive it.
* **Pause messages:** new messages in that chat are **held back**, with no alerts or counts, until you **Resume**. Then they all appear.

Your institution can switch any of these off.
